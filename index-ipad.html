Quick Reference

CFC
Chlorofluorocarbon. Ozone-depleting, phased out for new production.
HCFC
Hydrochlorofluorocarbon. Lower ODP than CFCs, still ozone-depleting.
HFC
Hydrofluorocarbon. No chlorine, zero ODP, often high GWP.
HFO
Hydrofluoroolefin. Low GWP, often mildly flammable A2L.
Natural
Ammonia, CO2, hydrocarbons, water, and air. Often low GWP but require safety-specific handling.
